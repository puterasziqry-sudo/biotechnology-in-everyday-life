Environmental Biotechnology

See how biological systems can contribute to wastewater treatment, bioremediation, resource recovery and environmental management.

PLANET

Using biology to manage environmental problems.

Environmental biotechnology uses microorganisms, enzymes, plants and biological processes to help address pollution, wastewater, waste and resource recovery.

Applications must still be engineered, monitored and evaluated for their environmental performance.

01

Wastewater treatment

Microbial communities can break down organic matter and help remove pollutants in treatment systems.

02

Bioremediation

Selected organisms or biological processes can help transform or remove certain contaminants.

03

Resource recovery

Biological systems can contribute to recovering nutrients, energy or useful materials from waste streams.

Important: environmental biotechnology is not automatically risk-free. Introduced organisms, altered ecosystems and waste-treatment systems still require appropriate controls, monitoring and risk assessment.
