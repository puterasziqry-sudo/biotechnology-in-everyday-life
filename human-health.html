HEALTHCARE

Biology can become medicine.

Biotechnology supports vaccines, diagnostic tests, therapeutic proteins, monoclonal antibodies, cell-based therapies and other biological products.

Many biological medicines are produced using living cells or biological systems, followed by purification, testing and quality control.

Example visual: vaccine technology and biological medicines.
01

Vaccines

Vaccines train the immune system to recognise specific targets. Biotechnology has enabled different vaccine platforms and manufacturing approaches.

02

Diagnostics

Molecular and immunological methods can detect biological markers, pathogens or changes associated with disease.

03

Biological medicines

Products such as monoclonal antibodies and other biologics can target specific molecules or pathways.
