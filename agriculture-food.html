FOOD & FARMING

From fermentation to improved crops.

Agricultural biotechnology includes tools that change or select biological traits in crops, animals and microorganisms. Food biotechnology also includes long-established processes such as fermentation.

Genetic modification is only one part of the wider field. Risk assessment should consider the specific organism, trait, product and intended use.

Crop improvement

Breeding and biotechnology can target traits such as pest resistance, disease resistance, stress tolerance or nutritional composition.

Golden Rice

Golden Rice was developed to produce beta-carotene in the grain. It is often used to explain how genetic engineering can target a specific nutritional trait.

Fermentation

Yeast and bacteria have long been used to make foods and beverages. Modern biotechnology can optimise microbes and enzymes for food and industrial processes.

Questions worth asking

  • What trait was changed or selected?
  • What is the intended benefit?
  • What evidence supports safety and effectiveness?
  • What environmental or socioeconomic effects should be considered?
