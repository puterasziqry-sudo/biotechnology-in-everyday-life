OUR OBJECTIVES

Why we built this website

  • Make biotechnology easier for non-specialists to understand.
  • Connect scientific principles to everyday examples.
  • Reduce misconceptions through evidence-based explanations.
  • Promote responsible discussion of benefits, risks and ethics.
  • Provide credible sources for further reading.
